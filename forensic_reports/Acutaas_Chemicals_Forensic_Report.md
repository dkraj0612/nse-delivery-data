**2.2 Raw Material Dynamics & Supply Chain Vulnerabilities**
Raw material costs represent a significant portion of the company's expenses, forming approximately 72% of the total cost of sales.

*   **Sourcing Risk:** Acutaas has a robust domestic sourcing strategy, with 71% of its raw materials sourced from India, and only 21% imported from China as of early 2026. This high degree of domestic sourcing acts as a substantial buffer against global supply chain disruptions and geopolitical risks. The company also manufactures over 90% of its advanced intermediates in-house, further reducing external supply shock exposure.
*   **FX Fluctuations:** As a net exporter, Acutaas is exposed to foreign exchange fluctuations. To mitigate this, the company revises prices for export sales quarterly, taking into account key starting material (KSM) prices and foreign exchange rates.
*   **Pricing Power:** The ability to revise export prices quarterly and conduct domestic sales on a spot basis provides some pricing power, allowing the company to pass on raw material price increases, albeit with a potential lag for domestic sales. The company's strategic shift towards higher-value molecules and increased CDMO share has also contributed to margin expansion, indicating improving pricing power.
